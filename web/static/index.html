MARKET SENTIMENT

NIFTY

FRESH DATA
1. Put-Call Ratio (PCR): Counts total Put OI divided by Call OI. If high (>1.3), institutions are writing Puts (bullish support floor). If low (<0.8), they are writing Calls (bearish resistance ceiling).
2. Max Pain: The strike where option buyers lose the most money. Spot prices are magnetically drawn towards this strike near expiry.
3. IV Skew: Call IV minus Put IV. If negative, puts are more expensive, indicating high demand for market protection (downside hedging/fear).
4. Action Signals:
  • BUY (Put Write): Floor support. Expect the market will hold above this strike price.
  • SELL (Put Cover): Support collapse. Put sellers are covering/running, indicating downside breakdown risk.
  • WATCH (Call Write): Ceiling resistance. Expect price consolidation or a breakout alert.
